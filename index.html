Clean Water & Sanitation for All

Together we can achieve Sustainable Development Goal 6 — ensuring availability and sustainable management of water and sanitation for everyone.

More than 40% of the world’s population already faces water scarcity, and this number is set to grow as global temperatures rise. While 2.1 billion people have gained access to safer sanitation since 1990, clean drinking water supplies are shrinking across every continent. With drought and desertification on the rise, water stress is becoming a global crisis. By 2050, one in four people could face recurring water shortages.

Water & Sanitation by the numbers

  • 0 71 percent of the global population, 5.2 billion people, had safely-managed drinking water in 2015, but 844 million people still lacked even basic drinking water.
  • 0 39 percent of the global population, 2.9 billion people, had safe sanitation in 2015, but 2.3 billion people still lacked basic sanitation. 892 million people practiced open defecation.
  • 0 80% of wastewater goes into waterways without adequate treatment.
  • 0 Water stress affects more than 2 billion people, with this figure projected to increase.
  • 0 80% of countries have laid the foundations for integrated water resources management.
  • 0 The world has lost 70% of its natural wetlands over the last century.
