About the Creator, Resources & Credits

Creator

This project was created by Azriel Williams, a student at the University of Trinidad and Tobago. as part of the Sustainable Development Goals awareness initiative. Passionate about technology, design, and making information accessible, this site was built to spread awareness of the importance of clean water and sanitation worldwide.
