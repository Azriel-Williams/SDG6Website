What is SG6?

Sustainable Development Goal 6 (SDG 6) focuses on “Clean Water and Sanitation.” It aims to make sure that everyone in the world has access to safe and affordable drinking water, proper sanitation services, and good hygiene. This goal also emphasizes protecting rivers, lakes, and groundwater from pollution and using water resources responsibly so they can last for future generations. Clean water and sanitation are important because they reduce disease, improve quality of life, and support education and economic growth. By achieving SDG 6, we can create healthier communities and a more sustainable world.

Why SDG 6 Matters

Water is essential for life and sustainable development. Without reliable access to clean water and sanitation, communities face severe health risks, economic setbacks, and environmental challenges. Achieving SDG 6 means securing a healthier, fairer, and more sustainable future for all.
